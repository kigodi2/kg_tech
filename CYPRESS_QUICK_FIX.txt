WHAT'S HAPPENING
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE=1:
  ❌ Electron runs in Node.js CLI mode
  ❌ Doesn't recognize Cypress's GUI options (--no-sandbox, --ping, etc.)
  ❌ Crashes with "bad option" errors

After fix (ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE= unsets it):
  ✅ Electron runs in normal GUI mode
  ✅ Recognizes all Cypress options
  ✅ Works perfectly
