================================================================================
KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
================================================================================

1. No Full Names in Templates
   → Minimizes personal data exposure per NECTA standards

2. School/Subject/Year-Specific Templates
   → Prevents accidental use of wrong template

3. Automatic Row Locking After Validation
   → Processed data becomes immutable (prevents human error)

4. Audit Logging of All Lock/Unlock Operations
   → Complete audit trail for examination accountability

5. SHA-256 Checksums for Integrity
   → Cryptographically secure, collision-resistant
