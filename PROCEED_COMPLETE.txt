HOW TO USE
================================================================================

1. Login to IRMS
2. Navigate to: /hierarchy/regions
3. Click on a Region to see its Districts
4. Click on a District to see its Schools
5. Click on a School to view complete Results Report

The results report includes:
  - Division Performance Summary (by gender)
  - Detailed Results Table (all candidates sorted by division & GPA)
  - Examination Centre Performance (school & subject analytics)
