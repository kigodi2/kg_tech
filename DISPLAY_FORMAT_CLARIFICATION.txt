TOTAL:
  Shows: Sum of all averages
  What:  Sum of all marks_obtained values
  Formula: 56.00 + 49.00 + 56.33 + 2.00 + 62.00 = 225.33

AVG:
  Shows: Average per subject
  What:  TOTAL ÷ number of subjects
  Formula: 225.33 ÷ 5 = 45.07

GRD:
  Shows: Best grade
  What:  Highest grade among all subjects
  Example: 'C' (if C is highest grade)

PTS:
  Shows: Total points
  What:  Sum of all grade points (for division calculation)
  Note:  Excludes GENERAL STUDIES and BASIC APPLIED MATHEMATICS

DIV:
  Shows: Final division
  What:  I, II, III, IV, or 0 (based on points)
  Based on: PTS values

GPA:
  Shows: Grade point average
  What:  PTS ÷ valid subjects
  Formula: 10 ÷ 5 = 2.0

POS:
  Shows: Position/Rank
  What:  Student's position in class
  Sorting: COMPLETE first, then ABS at bottom
