FOR DEVELOPERS
================================================================================

Issue: Alpine.js scope isolation via x-teleport

Fix:
  BEFORE: <template x-teleport="body"> ... </template>
  AFTER:  <div x-show=...> ... </div>

Why it works:
  • Modal stays in component DOM tree
  • Maintains scope chain
  • Functions accessible via this context
  • No more "undefined function" errors

Technical Details:
  See: SYSTEM_STACKING_FIX_SUMMARY_2026_02_15.md
