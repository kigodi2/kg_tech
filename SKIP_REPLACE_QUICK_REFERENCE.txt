❓ FAQ
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: Will existing candidates be deleted?
A: No. Skip mode ignores them. Replace only updates safe fields.

Q: Will marks be deleted?
A: No. Replace mode NEVER deletes marks, registrations, or results.

Q: What if I don't specify on_exists_mode?
A: Defaults to "skip" (safest behavior).

Q: Can I change exam_type in Replace mode?
A: No. exam_type is immutable to protect exam allocations.

Q: What fields are updated in Replace mode?
A: Only: full_name, gender, school_id

Q: What if duplicate candidate_id in file?
A: Second occurrence marked as ERROR, import blocked.

Q: Is it case-sensitive?
A: Yes. Only lowercase "skip" and "replace" accepted.
