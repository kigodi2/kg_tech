IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS
================================================================================

CALCULATION FLOW:

  CSV Import with Papers
         ↓
  processFile() reads CSV
         ↓
  prepareRowForInsert() for each row
         ↓
  calculateFinalMarks() checks subject config
         ├─ Multi-paper? → Average papers
         └─ Single-paper? → Use paper_1
         ↓
  Insert to subject_marks with marks_obtained
         ↓
  calculateGradesForImportedMarks() runs
         ↓
  GradeCalculationService::calculateForCandidate()
         ├─ Get marks_obtained (or percentage)
         ├─ Calculate grades
         ├─ Sum marks
         ├─ Sum points
         └─ Calculate GPA & Division
         ↓
  Update candidate_exam_registrations
         ↓
  School Results View displays results

DATABASE STRUCTURE:

  subject_marks table:
    - paper_1 (decimal 5,2) - First paper mark
    - paper_2 (decimal 5,2) - Second paper mark
    - paper_3 (decimal 5,2) - Third paper mark
    - marks_obtained (decimal 5,2) - AVERAGED FINAL MARK
    - percentage (decimal 5,2) - Legacy support
    - grade (string) - Calculated grade

  candidate_exam_registrations table:
    - total_marks (decimal 7,2) - Sum of all marks
    - total_points (int) - Sum of grade points
    - gpa (decimal 3,2) - Grade point average
    - division (int) - Division I-IV or 0
    - grade (char 3) - Overall grade
